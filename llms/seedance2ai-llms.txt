# Seedance 2.0 AI

> Online AI video, image, and music generation. Create 4–15 second videos from text, image, video, and audio references — keeping characters, style, motion, and sound consistent — plus studio-grade image generation and AI songs. Free to start, priced in credits.

Pages are published in 19 languages under a locale prefix (for example `/de/pricing`). The URLs below are the English defaults and carry the canonical tag; treat them as the primary version when citing this site.

## Video generation

- [Seedance 2.0 video generator](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/video/seedance2pro): Generate 4–15 second videos from text, image, video, and audio references, with character, style, motion, and sound kept consistent across shots.
- [Seedance 2.0 Mini](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/video/seedance2mini): The fastest and lowest-cost tier of Seedance 2.0, for drafts and high-volume iteration.
- [Seedance 2.5 video generator](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/video/seedance25): Longer-form generation, up to 30 seconds, with 4K output.

## Image generation

- [GPT Image 2](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/gpt-image-2): Studio-grade image generation for social posts, cinematic photos, posters, and product visuals.
- [GPT Image 2 Combo Grid](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/gpt-image-2/combo): Generate a grid of variations from one prompt in a single run, for side-by-side comparison.
- [Nano Banana Pro](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/nano-banana-pro): Image generation with accurate text rendering inside the image — signage, posters, packaging, and UI mockups.
- [Nano Banana 2](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/nano-banana-2): Image generation that can ground a picture in current Google Search results — useful when the image has to reflect real, up-to-date facts.
- [Nano Banana 2 Lite](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/nano-banana-2-lite): A fast, lightweight image model for quick drafts and bulk generation.
- [Seedream 5](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/seedream5): General-purpose image generation.
- [Seedream 5 Pro](https://www.seedance2ai.io/app/seedream5pro): The higher-fidelity Seedream 5 tier, for final-quality output.
